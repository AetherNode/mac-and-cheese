Prices: Yahoo Finance, 18:10 London (Nikkei: 24 Nov close). Rates, spreads and dollar: FRED, to 20 to 24 Nov. Positioning: CFTC, 17 Nov. Oil stocks: EIA, week to 20 Nov.

Method

Mac score: each driver votes up, down or neutral against its own recent trend, VIX counts double, summed and scaled to −10 to +10. Lean at 2 or more either way, strong at 4 or more.

Cheese score: price against its 50-day and 200-day averages, the 50-day against the 200-day, and the 20-day and 5-day moves, scaled to −10 to +10. It never feeds the Mac score.

Board reading: the average of every asset's Mac and Cheese scores.

Tiles: solid when both scores are 4 or more the same way, outline when weaker or one-sided, yellow when they conflict.

Not covered: options skew and CVOL, futures curve, gold ETF flows, PMIs and consensus forecasts, seasonality, UK and euro-area releases. Oil rests mainly on the shared backdrop plus COT and EIA stocks.

Bank of England and quarterly expiry dates are provisional.
